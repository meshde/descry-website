Why Descry

A flat diff tells you what changed. Not why.

Reviewing a 180-file PR as a wall of red and green lines doesn't scale — people skim, rubber-stamp, and bugs ship. Descry decomposes the change into the logical paths it actually took through your code.

⇶

Flow-based walkthroughs

Every hunk is grouped into a named flow and ordered into steps, so you review "what this PR does" instead of "which files it touched."

◎

Chain diagrams

See the real call chain behind a flow as a diagram. Click any node to jump straight to that function's own diff.

✦

Ask Descry

Chat scoped to a line, a step, or a whole flow — grounded in the actual code, not a generic summary.

⇄

Diff / Split / Base / PR views

Switch between an annotated diff, side-by-side split, or the plain base/PR file, per step, without losing your place.

✎

Anchored comments

Leave a comment on the exact lines it's about, track it, and resolve it once it's addressed.

⌁

Works with your coding agent

Claude Code and Codex can generate the walkthrough themselves right after implementing a plan, and answer your questions on it live.
